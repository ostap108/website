What I do

My clinical background is in DBT delivered in psychiatric settings, including work with adolescents presenting with severe clinical needs. I lead a DBT team, teach psychology at KSE, and help manage psychology training programs there. In 2019 I founded the Ukrainian affiliate of ACBS, the Association for Contextual Behavioral Science.

Current work

Alongside clinical work, I'm building training and credentialing infrastructure for evidence-based psychotherapy in Ukraine — including translating DBT AC-I materials into Ukrainian and developing sustainable professional pathways for Ukrainian clinicians. More detail on these projects is coming to this site soon.
