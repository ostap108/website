About

My background is in clinical and social psychology, with training that runs from psychiatric inpatient work through to academic teaching and organization-building.

Credentials & affiliations

  • Team lead, Ukrainian DBT team
  • Faculty, Kyiv School of Economics (KSE) — psychology
  • Founder, ACBS Ukraine (Association for Contextual Behavioral Science)
  • Clinical experience in psychiatric settings, including adolescents with severe clinical presentations

Approach

I work primarily within Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT) and Radically Open DBT (RO DBT) — evidence-based approaches to difficulties with emotion regulation, from everyday overwhelm to more severe presentations. Sessions are available in Ukrainian and English.
